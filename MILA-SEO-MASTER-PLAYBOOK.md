## 3. CONTENT STRATEGY

### What Google Says (Helpful Content System)

Google's helpful content system rewards content that is:
- **Written for humans first** (not to manipulate search rankings)
- **Demonstrating first-hand experience** (you actually do this work)
- **Providing a satisfying experience** (reader gets what they came for)

Google penalizes content that:
- Is thin or duplicated across pages
- Exists only to rank for keywords
- Doesn't add value beyond what's already available

### Blog Content Calendar (First 3 Months)

#### Month 1 — Foundation Content (4 posts)

**Post 1:** "What Is CARB Testing and Does My Truck Need It?"
- Target keyword: "what is CARB testing"
- Word count: 1000-1500
- Angle: Explain CARB testing in plain English. Who needs it, what happens during a test, what to expect. Include your experience as a tester.
- CTA: "Not sure if your vehicle needs testing? Text us your VIN and we'll tell you in 5 minutes."

**Post 2:** "CARB Compliance Deadlines 2026: What Fleet Owners Must Know"
- Target keyword: "CARB compliance deadlines 2026"
- Word count: 1200-1800
- Angle: Break down every relevant deadline. Make it a reference page people bookmark.
- CTA: "Need to get your fleet compliant before the deadline? We can test your entire fleet in one visit."

**Post 3:** "Mobile vs. Shop CARB Testing: Which Saves You More Money?"
- Target keyword: "mobile CARB testing vs shop"
- Word count: 800-1200
- Angle: Cost comparison. Factor in downtime, towing, scheduling flexibility.
- CTA: "Get a free quote for on-site testing. No downtime, no tow trucks."

**Post 4:** "How to Prepare Your Diesel Truck for Emissions Testing"

**Post 7:** "5 Signs Your Diesel Truck Will Fail an Emissions Test"
- How-to / list post format
- Based on your real testing experience

### Content Writing Rules

1. **Write from experience.** Start posts with "In my X years of testing..." or "Last week on a job in Sacramento..."
2. **Include specific numbers.** "$10,000 per vehicle per day" is more powerful than "significant fines"
3. **Use the inverted pyramid.** Answer the question in the first paragraph. Details after.
4. **One primary keyword per post.** Use it in the H1, first paragraph, one H2, and naturally 2-3 more times.
5. **Add images with alt text.** Every post needs at least one image. Alt text = describe the image + include keyword if natural.
6. **Internal link to service pages.** Every blog post should link to at least one service page.
7. **End with a clear CTA.** Tell them exactly what to do next. Phone number, text number, or booking link.

### What Google Says (Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines)

E-E-A-T = **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness**

This is how Google's human quality raters evaluate content. It's not a direct ranking algorithm, but it heavily influences what Google considers "high quality."

For a CARB testing business, here's how to demonstrate each:

#### Experience (MOST IMPORTANT for your business)
- **Show you actually do this work.** Job site photos, testing in action, real results.
- Blog posts starting with "In my experience testing fleets across Sacramento..."

#### Expertise
- Mention certifications, training, years of experience
- Create an "About" page with Bryan's background

#### Authoritativeness

## 7. CORE WEB VITALS

### What Google Measures

| Metric | Target | What It Means |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| **LCP** (Largest Contentful Paint) | < 2.5 seconds | How fast the main content loads |
| **INP** (Interaction to Next Paint) | < 200 milliseconds | How fast the page responds to clicks |
| **CLS** (Cumulative Layout Shift) | < 0.1 | How much the page jumps around while loading |

### How to Check
1. Go to [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/)
2. Enter norcalcarbmobile.com
3. Target: 90+ score on mobile

### Common Fixes
- **Slow LCP:** Compress images (use WebP format), use lazy loading, minimize CSS
- **High INP:** Reduce JavaScript, defer non-critical scripts
- **High CLS:** Set width/height on all images, don't insert content above existing content

### For Squarespace Specifically
- Use Squarespace's built-in image optimizer
- Limit custom code injections
- Don't add heavy third-party widgets
- Use system fonts when possible (faster than custom fonts)

## 9. AI CONTENT POLICY

### What Google Says (Official Position, Updated 2025)

Google does NOT penalize AI-generated content. They penalize **low-quality content regardless of how it was made.**

Their policy: "Rewarding high-quality content, however it is produced."

### Rules for Using Mila/AI for Content

1. **AI drafts, Bryan edits.** Mila can write the first draft. Bryan adds real experience, specific examples, and personal voice.
2. **Add first-hand experience.** Every AI-drafted post needs at least 2-3 specific details only Bryan would know.
3. **Fact-check everything.** AI can get CARB regulations wrong. Always verify against carb.ca.gov.
4. **Don't mass-produce.** Quality over quantity. 4 great posts per month beats 20 thin ones.
5. **Author attribution.** Blog posts should be "By Bryan [Last Name], NorCal Carb Mobile" — not "By AI."
