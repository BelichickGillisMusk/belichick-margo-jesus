Key Deadlines

Stay ahead of CARB compliance deadlines. Tests can be submitted up to 90 days early.

2025-26 2x

Semi-Annual Testing

All non-agricultural vehicles and non-CA motorhomes must be tested twice per year. Deadlines are based on your DMV registration expiration date and 6 months after.

OCT 2027

Quarterly Testing Begins

OBD-equipped vehicles (2013+ diesel, 2018+ alt fuel) must test four times per year. Start planning now for increased testing frequency.

ANNUAL 1x

Agricultural Vehicles

Vehicles that meet CARB's agricultural vehicle definition are tested once per year. Same applies to California-registered motorhomes used for recreation or emergency.

WITHIN 30d

New Vehicle Reporting

Any newly acquired vehicle must be reported in CTC-VIS within 30 calendar days of acquisition. Selling? Update within 30 days too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Clean Truck Check compliance.

Nearly ALL diesel, alternative fuel, and hybrid vehicles with a Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) over 14,000 pounds operating on California roads. This includes commercial trucks, buses, government vehicles, and even privately-owned vehicles. Out-of-state vehicles operating in California are also subject to the program.
No. Out-of-state vehicles are NOT exempt. Any vehicle over 14,000 lbs GVWR that operates on California public roads must comply with Clean Truck Check, regardless of where it's registered. Your compliance deadlines are based on the last digit of your VIN.
No. There is NO low-use exemption, and small fleets (including single-vehicle fleets) are NOT exempt. Every subject vehicle must be registered, pay the annual compliance fee, and pass emissions testing.
It depends on your engine year. Vehicles with 2013+ diesel engines or 2018+ alternative fuel engines need an OBD data scan using a CARB-certified device. Pre-2013 diesel engines need a smoke opacity test (SAE J1667) plus a visual inspection. Pre-2018 alternative fuel engines need a visual inspection only.

Penalties can reach up to $10,000 per vehicle per day for non-compliance. CARB also places DMV registration holds on non-compliant vehicles (including DMV-exempt plates). Out-of-state trucks may be restricted from operating in California. CARB uses roadside monitoring with remote emissions devices and license plate readers to flag non-compliant vehicles.
For 2025 and onward, the annual compliance fee is $31.18 per vehicle (adjusted annually by California CPI). This must be paid through CTC-VIS — payments through any other system do not count. This fee is separate from and in addition to emissions testing costs.
