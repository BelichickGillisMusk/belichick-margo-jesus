For San Diego market testing, newer OBD-equipped diesel vehicles usually need an OBD scan with a CARB-certified device. Dispatch still confirms before booking.

CARB-backed rules of thumb

  • OBD-equipped vehicles generally use a CARB-certified OBD test device.
  • 2012 and older diesel engines generally need smoke opacity testing plus a visual inspection.
  • Passing tests may be submitted up to 90 days before the vehicle compliance deadline.
  • Vehicle owners should always confirm due dates in CTC-VIS.

How much does mobile CARB testing cost in San Diego?

HD-OBD testing is $119 and OVI smoke opacity testing is $219 per vehicle for San Diego County. Fleet routes and multi-truck yards may qualify for bundled scheduling.

Do you come to our yard or job site?

Yes. Mobile appointments at fleet yards, warehouses, contractor lots, farms, and job sites across San Diego County.

Are you the same company as NorCal CARB Mobile?

Sibling brand, same tester network. NorCal covers Northern California. This page is San Diego County only (OBD $119 · OVI $219).

What is the difference between HD-OBD and OVI smoke testing?

HD-OBD reads supported onboard diagnostic data, commonly for 2013+ diesel. OVI smoke / opacity is for older diesel that needs a visible emissions opacity check. See generic OBD and generic OVI pages.
