What OVI Testing Is

OVI means Opacity/Visual Inspection. We use an SAE J1667 smoke meter and verify emissions control equipment visually so your result meets CARB heavy-duty testing requirements.

🚛

Who Needs It

Pre-2013 diesel engines, motorhomes, and any vehicle that cannot use OBD typically need OVI testing instead of a data scan.
