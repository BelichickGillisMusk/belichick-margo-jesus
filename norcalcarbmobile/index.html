Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CARB Clean Truck Check compliance testing.

Clean Truck Check is California's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance (HD I/M) program under CARB. It requires emissions testing for all diesel, alternative fuel, and hybrid vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR operating on California roads. Testing includes OBD compliance scans for newer engines and smoke opacity tests for older diesels.
Nearly all diesel, alternative fuel, and hybrid vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR operating in California. This includes out-of-state trucks passing through, owner-operators, small fleets, agricultural vehicles, and government vehicles. There is no low-use exemption and no small fleet exemption.
Our mobile OBD compliance scan is $85 per vehicle. Smoke opacity tests are $200 per vehicle. Fleet discounts are available for 5 or more vehicles. Because we come to you, there are no tow or transport costs — and zero downtime for your operations.
No. NorCal Carb Mobile is a fully mobile testing service. We come to your yard, depot, distribution center, or job site anywhere in Northern California. CARB does not require a brick-and-mortar station for heavy-duty emissions testing. This means zero downtime for your fleet.
Non-compliant vehicles face fines up to $10,000 per vehicle per day. CARB also enforces through DMV registration holds (your truck can't renew registration), roadside remote emissions monitoring devices, and automated license plate readers at ports and weigh stations.
Currently, vehicles must be tested twice per year (semi-annual). Starting October 2027, OBD-equipped vehicles will move to quarterly testing — four times per year. Agricultural vehicles currently test once per year. Staying ahead of these deadlines is critical to avoiding penalties.
Every covered vehicle must pay an annual compliance fee of $31.18 through the CTC-VIS portal. This is separate from testing costs and is required regardless of test results. We can help you navigate the CTC-VIS portal, register your vehicles, and process fee payments.
We serve Sacramento, Placer, El Dorado, Yolo, San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Contra Costa, and Alameda counties. That covers the greater Sacramento region, the Central Valley corridor, and the East Bay. Extended service is available by appointment.
