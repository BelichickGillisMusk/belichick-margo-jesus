Frequently Asked Questions

Everything fleet operators and owner-operators need to know about CARB testing.

Clean Truck Check is California's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance (HD I/M) program under AB-210. It requires emissions testing for nearly all diesel and alternative fuel vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR operating on California roads. Testing includes OBD compliance scans for newer vehicles and smoke opacity tests for older diesel engines.

Nearly all diesel, alternative fuel, and hybrid vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR operating in California. This includes trucks, buses, motorhomes, agricultural vehicles, and out-of-state vehicles. There is no low-use exemption and no small fleet exemption.

Our mobile OBD compliance scan starts at $85 per vehicle. Smoke opacity testing for pre-2013 diesel vehicles is $200. Fleet discounts are available for 5 or more vehicles. All prices include mobile service at your location — no extra travel fees in our service area.

No. NorCal Carb Mobile is fully mobile — we come to your yard, depot, job site, or wherever your vehicles are located. CARB does not require a brick-and-mortar station for HD I/M testing. Your trucks stay productive while we test on-site.

Non-compliant vehicles face fines of up to $10,000 per vehicle per day. CARB also enforces through DMV registration holds, roadside remote emissions monitoring devices (REMD), and automated license plate readers. The cost of compliance is far less than the cost of penalties.

Currently, vehicles must be tested twice per year (semi-annually). Starting October 2027, OBD-tested vehicles move to quarterly testing — four times per year. Agricultural vehicles currently test once per year. We track deadlines for fleet customers so you never miss a test.

CARB charges an annual compliance fee of $31.18 per vehicle starting in 2025. This is separate from the cost of testing and is paid through the CTC-VIS portal. We can help you navigate CTC-VIS registration and fee payment.

We serve Sacramento, Placer, El Dorado, Yolo, San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Contra Costa, and Alameda counties. Major cities include Sacramento, Roseville, Stockton, Modesto, Tracy, Lodi, Elk Grove, Davis, and Fairfield. Extended service areas available by appointment.
