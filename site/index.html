Security First

Good Claw / Bad Claw Architecture

Agents are free to think internally. But the moment they touch the outside world, Jon Jones reviews every action. Nothing gets out without approval.

🔒

Outbound Gating

Every email, Slack message, and API call is reviewed before it sends. PII, credentials, and legal risk are caught automatically.

🚨

Rate Limiting

Hard caps on emails (50/day), Slack posts (20/hr), and auto-approvals (50/hr). Circuit breakers kill runaway agents.

📝

Immutable Audit Logs

Every agent action is logged with timestamp, source, verdict, and content hash. 90-day retention. Full forensic trail.

🔐

Encrypted Vault

Sensitive files locked behind AES-256-GCM encryption. Human password required. No agent can unlock without you.

🛡️

Sandboxed Workspaces

Each agent operates in an isolated workspace. No cross-contamination. Jon Jones can read all for review but cannot modify.

⚠️

Human Escalation

High-risk actions (new contacts, invoices over $500, legal docs, bulk email) always require human approval. 60-min timeout auto-rejects.
