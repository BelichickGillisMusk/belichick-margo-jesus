## WHAT IS CLEAN TRUCK CHECK?

In December 2021, CARB approved the Heavy-Duty Vehicle Inspection and Maintenance (HD I/M) Regulation, now known as Clean Truck Check. It stems from Senate Bill 210 (Leyva; Chapter 298, Statutes of 2019), which directed CARB to develop a comprehensive inspection and maintenance program for heavy-duty vehicles.

Heavy-duty vehicles comprise only 3% of all vehicles on California roads but produce more than 50% of nitrogen oxides (NOx) and fine particle diesel pollution from all mobile sources in the state. By 2037, the program is estimated to reduce 82 tons per day of NOx and fine particle diesel pollution.

---

## WHICH VEHICLES ARE AFFECTED?

Clean Truck Check applies to nearly ALL diesel, alternative fuel, and hybrid vehicles with a Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) over 14,000 pounds operating on public roads and highways in California - even if not registered in California.

### Vehicles INCLUDED:
- Commercial trucks and tractors
- Buses (transit, shuttle, school, motorcoach)
- Hybrid trucks and buses
- Government vehicles (federal, state, local)
- Privately-owned/personal vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR
- California-registered motorhomes
- Single vehicle fleets
- Out-of-state vehicles operating in California
- Vehicles with CA DMV-exempt license plates

### Vehicles EXEMPT:
- Emergency vehicles (CA Vehicle Code section 165 or CA Health and Safety Code section 1797.84)
- Historical vehicles (CA Vehicle Code section 5004)
- New vehicles with engines certified to most stringent optional NOx standard (≤0.01 g/bhp-hr) - ONLY during first 4 years (2023-2027)
- Strictly off-road vehicles not required to be DMV registered

### NOT EXEMPT:
- There is NO low-use exemption
- Small fleets are NOT exempt
- Out-of-state vehicles are NOT exempt
- Personal vehicles over 14,000 lbs are NOT exempt

## THREE COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

### 1. REPORTING
- All subject vehicles must be reported in the Clean Truck Check Vehicle Inspection System (CTC-VIS)
- New vehicles must be reported within 30 calendar days of acquisition
- Vehicle owners must maintain accurate account information
- Must update vehicle info within 30 calendar days of buying or selling a vehicle

### 2. ANNUAL COMPLIANCE FEE
- 2024: $30.00 per vehicle
- 2025 onward: $31.18 per vehicle (adjusted annually per California CPI)
- Must be paid before the due date to remain compliant
- Payment must be made through CTC-VIS - payments through any other system do NOT count

### 3. EMISSIONS COMPLIANCE TESTING
- Effective October 1, 2024
- All compliance deadlines on or after January 1, 2025 require a passing emissions compliance test
- Must be performed by a CARB credentialed tester
- Passing tests may be submitted up to 90 days prior to the compliance deadline

---

## TESTING TYPES BY VEHICLE

### OBD-Equipped Vehicles (2013+ diesel engines, 2018+ alternative fuel engines)
- Must undergo OBD data scan
- Must use CARB-certified OBD testing device
- Using non-validated device = unsuccessful test

### Diesel Non-OBD Vehicles (2012 and older diesel engines)
- Must complete smoke opacity test (SAE J1667-compatible smoke meter required)
- Must complete visual inspection of emissions control equipment (Vehicle Emissions Control Equipment Inspection)

### Alternative Fuel Non-OBD Vehicles (2017 and older alternative fuel engines)
- Visual inspection ONLY
- NOT subject to smoke opacity test

---

## COMPLIANCE DEADLINES

### For California-Registered Vehicles:
- Based on DMV registration expiration date
- Semi-annual deadlines: DMV expiration date AND 6 months after
- Example: Registration expires January 1 → deadlines are January 1 and July 1

### For Out-of-State Vehicles:
- Based on last digit of Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)

### Testing Can Be Submitted Early:
- Up to 90 days before the compliance deadline

## TESTING FREQUENCY TIMELINE

| Period | Frequency | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| 2024 | Once | One-time test for vehicles subject to semi-annual compliance |
| 2025-2026 | Twice per year (semi-annual) | Non-agricultural vehicles and non-CA motorhomes |
| Oct 2027+ | Four times per year | ONLY for OBD-equipped vehicles |
| Agricultural vehicles | Once per year | Must meet agricultural vehicle definition |
| CA motorhomes (recreational/emergency) | Once per year | Must be CA-registered |

## PHASED IMPLEMENTATION

### Phase 1 (January 2023):
- Deployment of Remote Emissions Monitoring Devices (REMD)
- Automated License Plate Readers (ALPR)
- Screening for potential high emitters

### Phase 2 (October 2023):
- Vehicle owners must register vehicles in CTC-VIS database
- Initial annual compliance fee of $30 per vehicle

### Phase 3 (Originally Oct 2023, delayed to Jan 2025):
- Periodic emissions testing begins
- Similar to California's Smog Check for cars but for heavy-duty

---

## ENFORCEMENT

### DMV Registration Holds:
- Applied to non-compliant vehicles
- Includes vehicles with CA DMV-exempt plates
- CARB actively enforces registration holds

### Roadside Monitoring (since January 2023):
- REMD (Remote Emissions Monitoring Devices)
- ALPR (Automated License Plate Readers)
- Flags potential high emitters

### Notice to Submit to Testing (NST):
- Sent to owners of flagged vehicles
- 30 calendar days to submit a passing test by a credentialed tester

### Penalties:
- Up to $10,000 per vehicle per day for non-compliance
- Out-of-state trucks may be restricted from operating in California
- Disabling, removing, or tampering with emissions control systems = violations and fines
- Vehicles deemed non-compliant may be subject to additional testing

## FREIGHT CONTRACTORS & BROKERS

- Must only contract with compliant vehicles/fleets
- Must comply with record-keeping requirements
- Must verify compliance before entering agreements/contracts
- Can use the Affirmation of Fleetwide Compliance to streamline verification
- Subject vehicles must be reported and compliant before operating on CA roads

## CREDENTIALED TESTERS

### How to Become a Credentialed Tester:
1. Complete CARB's free online training course
2. Pass the accompanying exam (minimum 80% score)
3. Receive Tester ID (valid for 2 years)
4. No formal prerequisites required (diesel industry background helpful)
5. People from ANY state can become testers

### Renewal:
- Must retake training course and pass exam every 2 years

### Email Requirement:
- CTC-VIS tester account email MUST match OBD test device login email exactly

### Equipment Needed:
- OBD testing: CARB-certified OBD test device
- Smoke opacity: SAE J1667-compatible smoke meter

### Career Opportunities:
- Fleet in-house tester
- Service center tester
- Independent mobile inspection service
- Third-party testing business

### Alternative Training:
- CCDET (California Council on Diesel Education and Technology)
- Offers live classroom + hands-on instruction
- Still must take CARB's online exam after course
- Website: https://ccdet.org/class-schedule/

### Available for Hire Testers:
- Public list: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/inspection-and-maintenance-program/credentialed-hd-im-tester-public-list

---

## TESTING PROCESS (Remote Capable)

Unlike light-duty smog checks, there is NO requirement to go to a brick-and-mortar station. Heavy-duty vehicle owners can complete testing and submit data remotely without traveling to designated testing locations.
