## YouTube Formula

### Title (The Click)
Format: `[Power Word] + [Topic] + [Benefit/Number]`

Good: "CARB Fines Are $10,000/Day — Here's How to Avoid Them"
Good: "I Tested 50 Trucks in One Day — Here's What Failed"
Bad: "CARB Clean Truck Check Information Video"

Rules:
- Under 60 characters
- Number or dollar amount in title when possible
- Promise a specific outcome
- Front-load the keyword

### Thumbnail (The Sell)
- One face with strong emotion OR one dramatic image
- 3-5 words MAX on thumbnail (readable on mobile)
- High contrast colors (yellow/black, red/white)
- Show the OUTCOME not the process
- Before/after or problem/solution split

### Hook (First 8 Seconds)
Open with the most interesting thing in the video:
- "This truck just got a $47,000 fine. Here's why."
- "I'm about to test this fleet — and the owner has no idea if they'll pass."
- "Starting October 2027, you'll need FOUR tests a year. Let me explain."

NEVER: "Hey guys, welcome to my channel, today we're going to..."

### Script Structure
```
HOOK (0-8s): Most interesting claim/visual
PROBLEM (8-30s): Why this matters to the viewer
PROOF (30s-2m): Evidence, stats, examples
SOLUTION (2-4m): What to do about it
CTA (last 15s): Subscribe + "Call us for testing"
```

### SEO Package (Every Video Gets This)
- Title: keyword-optimized, under 60 chars
- Description: 200+ words, keyword in first sentence, links, timestamps
- Tags: 15-20 relevant tags, mix broad + specific
- Chapters: timestamps for every section
- Cards: link to related videos at key moments
- End screen: subscribe + best related video
- Pinned comment: CTA with phone number / website

## Blog Content (Squarespace)

### Writing Rules
- First-hand experience beats generic info
- Specific numbers > vague claims ("$10,000/day" not "significant fines")
- Inverted pyramid: answer first, details after
- One primary keyword per post
- Every post has: image with alt text, internal links, clear CTA
- 800-1500 words. Not longer unless the topic demands it.
- AI drafts, Bryan edits and adds personal experience

### Monthly Blog Calendar
- Week 1: Publish post + GBP update
- Week 2: Social media pushes from blog content
- Week 3: Publish second post + citation submission
- Week 4: Review metrics + plan next month
