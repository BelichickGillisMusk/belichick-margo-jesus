## Key CARB Programs to Monitor for Business Opportunities

### 1. Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)
- 2025 LCFS Amendment Implementation FAQ
- New sustainability requirements effective July 1, 2025
- Compliance requirements for 2026 data year
- URL: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/fact-sheets/2025-lcfs-amendment-implementation-faq

### 2. Cap-and-Trade Program
- 2026 Annual Allowance Price data
- Compliance instrument reports
- Vintage 2025/2026 allowance allocations
- URL: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/cap-and-trade-program/cap-and-trade-program-data

### 3. CARB Research Funding ($4-8M/year)
- FY 2025-2026 Research Project Solicitation
- ~10-12 projects funded annually
- Topics: soil emissions, cooking emissions, air toxics, wildfire smoke
- Projects begin spring 2026, 24-month duration
- URL: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/documents/carb-fiscal-year-2025-2026-research-project-solicitation

### 4. Sustainable Transportation & Communities
- FY 2025-2026 pre-proposal solicitations
- Six priority research projects
- Open to CSU and UC academics
- URL: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/sustainable-communities-program/stcd-research-solicitations

### 5. Planning & Capacity Building Grants
- 2025 RFA open December 2, 2025
- Eligible: CBOs, Tribal Governments, Local Governments, Public Schools
- Must benefit disadvantaged or low-income areas
- URL: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/pcb-rfa

### 6. Federal Air Permitting Changes
- EPA relaxation of NSR and Title V permitting
- California districts need guidance
- Potential gap in compliance services
- URL: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/documents/us-epas-relaxation-federal-air-permitting

### 7. Advanced Clean Trucks (ACT) Regulation
- Manufacturers building credit banks for 2025-2026 requirements
- HD Omnibus regulation
- Legacy engine provisions 2024-2026 transition
- ZEV transition creating new service needs

## CARB Services Portal (ww2.arb.ca.gov/services)

### REGISTER
- **DOORS** - Off-Road Regulations Reporting Tool (off-road equipment compliance)
- **PERP** - Portable Equipment Registration Program (portable engines, generators, compressors)
- **TRUCRS** - Truck Regulation Upload, Compliance and Reporting System (truck/bus fleet compliance)

### REPORT
- **The TruckStop** - Central hub for all truck/fleet regulations
- **Air Pollution Complaints** - Public complaint submission
- **Prescribed Fires** - Burn reporting

### STAY CONNECTED
- **Subscribe** - CARB mailing lists and updates
- **Training Courses** - Including credentialed tester training
- **Submit Comments** - Public comment on regulations
- **Events** - CARB workshops, hearings, meetings
- **Request a Speaker** - CARB outreach
- **Calendar** - Regulatory calendar and deadlines

### SEARCH
- **Aftermarket, Performance, and Add-On Parts** - CARB-certified parts database
- **Breathe Well** - Air quality tools
- **Facility Emissions** - Emissions data lookup

### FUNDING PROGRAMS
- **Carl Moyer Program** - Clean air incentive funding for cleaner equipment
- **Goods Movement Emission Reduction Program** - Funding for emission reduction projects
- **School Bus Retrofit & Replacement Account** - Funding to replace/retrofit old school buses

## Business Opportunities Identified from CARB Programs

### OPPORTUNITY 1: Mobile Credentialed Testing Service
- ANYONE can become a credentialed tester (free CARB training)
- No brick-and-mortar location required
- Testing can be done remotely/on-site
- Every truck over 14,000 lbs in CA needs testing 2-4x/year
- Out-of-state trucks entering CA also need testing
- Startup cost: OBD device + smoke meter ($2,000-5,000)

### OPPORTUNITY 2: Compliance Management Software/Service
- Fleets need to track compliance deadlines per vehicle
- Freight brokers must verify compliance before contracting
- CTC-VIS management for multi-vehicle fleets
- Recurring revenue per vehicle managed

### OPPORTUNITY 3: LCFS Credit Trading Consultancy
- New 2025 LCFS amendments create compliance complexity
- Small businesses need help understanding credit obligations
- True-up credits, CI exceedance deficits, MOS provisions

### OPPORTUNITY 4: Grant Writing for CARB Funding
- $4-8M in annual research funding
- Planning & capacity building grants
- Sustainable transportation grants
- Small orgs need help applying

### OPPORTUNITY 5: ZEV Transition Services
- Advanced Clean Trucks regulation pushing fleet electrification
- Charging infrastructure planning
- Fleet transition consulting
- Legacy engine compliance during 2024-2026 transition period

### OPPORTUNITY 6: TRUCRS / DOORS / PERP Registration Service
- Many owner-operators struggle with multiple CARB reporting systems
- TRUCRS, DOORS, PERP each have their own requirements
- One-stop compliance registration service
- Help fleets register across ALL required CARB systems
- Recurring revenue: annual renewals and reporting

### OPPORTUNITY 7: Carl Moyer Program Grant Assistance
- Carl Moyer Program provides incentive FUNDING for cleaner equipment
- Many truckers don't know this money exists
- Help them apply for grants to replace/retrofit equipment
- Take a percentage or flat fee for successful applications
- Win-win: trucker gets funded, you get paid

### OPPORTUNITY 8: School Bus Retrofit & Replacement
- Dedicated state funding for school bus fleet modernization
- School districts need help navigating CARB requirements
- Grant writing + project management for bus replacement
- EV school bus transition consulting

### OPPORTUNITY 9: Aftermarket Parts Compliance Consulting
- CARB certifies aftermarket/performance parts
- Shops and distributors need to verify parts are CARB-legal
- Help shops audit their parts inventory for compliance
- Especially relevant for diesel performance/tuning shops

### OPPORTUNITY 10: CARB Training & Education Business
- Credentialed tester training (beyond the free CARB course)
- CCDET-style hands-on training programs
- Fleet owner compliance workshops
- Freight broker obligation training
- Spanish-language compliance training (HUGE underserved market)
