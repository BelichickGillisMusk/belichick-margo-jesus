# Legal Research Sources

## Federal
- Congress.gov - Bills and laws: https://www.congress.gov/
- eCFR - Code of Federal Regulations: https://www.ecfr.gov/
- Federal Register - New rules: https://www.federalregister.gov/
- SBA - Small business regulations: https://www.sba.gov/

## Illinois State
- ILGA - Illinois General Assembly: https://www.ilga.gov/
- Illinois Compiled Statutes: https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs.asp
- IL Secretary of State - Business filings: https://www.ilsos.gov/
- IDFPR - Professional licensing: https://idfpr.illinois.gov/

## Chicago / Cook County
- Chicago Municipal Code: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chicago/latest/overview
- Chicago BACP - Business licensing: https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/bacp.html
- Cook County Clerk - Business registration: https://www.cookcountyclerk.com/
- Chicago Zoning: https://secondcityzoning.org/

## Industry-Specific
- FDA (food, supplements, health): https://www.fda.gov/
- FTC (advertising, marketing): https://www.ftc.gov/
- SEC (securities, crypto): https://www.sec.gov/
- FCC (communications, tech): https://www.fcc.gov/

## Business Opportunity Research
- Search for: "new regulation [industry]" + "compliance requirement"
- Search for: "[city] licensing requirement [business type]"
- Search for: "regulatory barrier [industry] small business"
- Search for: "[state] recently passed law business impact"
