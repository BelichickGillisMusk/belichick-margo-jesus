## Deductions Checklist — Mobile CARB Testing Business

### Vehicle (HUGE for this business)
```
METHOD 1: Standard Mileage Rate (2026: check IRS.gov for current rate)
- Track EVERY business mile: to/from testing sites, equipment pickup, bank, post office
- Keep a mileage log (date, destination, purpose, miles)
- Apps: MileIQ, Everlance, or simple spreadsheet

METHOD 2: Actual Expenses
- Gas, oil, tires, insurance, registration, repairs, car wash
- Depreciation (Section 179 or MACRS)
- Loan interest on business vehicle
- Only deduct business-use percentage

WHICH IS BETTER?
- Calculate both. Use whichever gives the larger deduction.
- For a truck/van used primarily for business, actual expenses usually wins.
- Section 179 can deduct the FULL purchase price of a work vehicle in year 1
  (up to the limit — check current year's cap)
```

## IRS Audit Red Flags — Avoid These

1. **No mileage log** — #1 audit trigger for vehicle deductions. KEEP A LOG.
2. **100% business use on vehicle** — Almost nobody uses a vehicle 100% for business. Be honest.
3. **Round numbers everywhere** — $5,000 exactly for gas screams "I guessed." Track actuals.
4. **Home office for renters** — Perfectly legal, but document the dedicated space.
5. **Large Schedule C losses** — If business shows losses year after year, IRS may reclassify as "hobby."
6. **No 1099s filed** — If you pay contractors and don't file 1099s, IRS will notice.
7. **Mixing personal and business expenses** — Separate bank accounts. Always.
8. **Cash income not reported** — Report ALL income. IRS matches 1099s to your return.
